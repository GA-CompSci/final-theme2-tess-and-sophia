Gear Guide

Must Have
  • Duffle Bag
  • Sleeping Bag
  • Sleeping Pad
  • Light Source
  • Rain Suit
  • Good Boots
  • Wool or Synthetic Hiking Socks (no cotton)
  • Mess kit (with mug or cup)
  • Water Bottle
Get Soon
  • Backpack
  • Polypropylene Long Underwear
  • Hammock or Tent
Nice to Have
  • Backpacking Stove
  • Pocket Knife
  • Compass
  • Sleeping Bag Liner or Blanket
  • Bug Repellant
  • Paracord and Carabiners
Where to Buy
  • Campmor.com is a good discount camping catalog that offers an additional 10% off if you buy through the crew.
  • REI is a medium-end camping supplier. As a member-owned company, you can earn rebates on your purchases at REI.
  • REI Outlet often has great discounts on discontinued models.
  • Walmart generally has low-end camping gear - but sometimes low end is good enough, especially for gear that is easily lost or broken.
  • The Backpackers Shop in Sheffiled Village has an excellent selection and terrific staff who will answer all your questions. Strongly recommended for any gear that needs sizing.
  • Appalachian Outfitters in Peninsula is another excellent store for trying on gear and getting all your questions answered.

Cautions:

  • Cabela's in Avon sells primarly to hunters and other stationary outdoorsmen. Their gear is sturdy but very, very heavy. Army Surpus gear is the same.
  • Dick's Sporting Goods tends to be low quality and heavy.
